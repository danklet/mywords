MyWords

A password manager that keeps your vault on your device.

No account. No cloud. No tracking. Your passwords, cards, and secure notes are encrypted on your iPhone or iPad and never sent to us — because there is no “us” to send them to. We run no servers.

What it does

  • Everything in one encrypted vault — logins, secure notes, credit cards, bank accounts, driver licenses and passports, with encrypted file, photo, and scanned-document attachments.
  • AutoFill — your logins appear above the keyboard in Safari and in your apps, after Face ID or Touch ID confirms it's you.
  • Real encryption — AES-256-GCM with PBKDF2 key derivation, an optional Secret Key, auto-lock, and a clipboard that clears itself.
  • A security audit that flags weak passwords, reused passwords, and anything you haven't changed in over a year.
  • Password and passphrase generation with a live strength meter.
  • Direct sync with the MyWords desktop app over your own Wi-Fi, end-to-end encrypted, with nothing in between.
  • Encrypted backups you keep wherever you like.
